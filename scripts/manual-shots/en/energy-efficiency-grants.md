# Riverside City Energy Efficiency Grant Guidelines

### Amount of the Grant
- The amount of the grant is the eligible cost $C$ multiplied by two-thirds, $S = \frac{2}{3}C$.
- The amount under the preceding paragraph is capped at the amount calculated by the following formula.
  ```math
  \text{Cap} = \min\left( S,\ 1{,}000{,}000 \right)
  ```
- Any fraction of less than 1,000 in the amount calculated under the preceding two paragraphs is rounded down.
  $$S' = 1000 \left\lfloor \frac{S}{1000} \right\rfloor$$

### Grant Rate
- The grant rate is as follows.
  ```math
  r = \begin{cases} \dfrac{2}{3} & (C < 3{,}000{,}000) \\[4pt] \dfrac{1}{2} & (C \ge 3{,}000{,}000) \end{cases}
  ```
