## General Provisions

### Purpose {#purpose}
- This Employee Handbook (the "Handbook") sets out the working conditions and rules of conduct for employees of Acme Corp. (the "Company").
- Matters not covered by this Handbook are governed by applicable employment laws and regulations.

### Scope {#scope}
- This Handbook applies to all employees of the Company.
- Separate rules apply to part-time employees.
- Matters not covered by those separate rules are governed by this Handbook.

### Compliance
- The Company provides work under the conditions set out in this Handbook, and employees shall comply with this Handbook.

## Hiring and Transfers

### Hiring Procedure
- The Company selects applicants through interviews and other assessments and hires those who pass.

### Documents to Submit
- New employees shall submit the following documents within two weeks after the date of hire:
  - Proof of address
  - A copy of their driver's license (if they hold one)
  - Copies of professional certificates (if they hold any)
  - Any other documents the Company designates
- Employees shall notify the Company in writing without delay of any change to the information in those documents.

### Probationary Period
- New employees are subject to a probationary period of three months from the date of hire.
- The Company may shorten or waive the probationary period if it finds it appropriate.
- The Company may dismiss an employee found unsuitable during the probationary period, following the procedure in [[dismissal]].
- The probationary period counts toward length of service.

### Statement of Working Conditions
- When hiring an employee, the Company shall provide a written statement of wages, place of work, duties, working hours, days off and other working conditions, together with this Handbook.

### Transfers
- The Company may order an employee to change their place of work or duties for business reasons.
- Employees shall not refuse a transfer order without a justifiable reason.

### Leave of Absence
- The Company may grant a leave of absence to an employee who is unable to work because of illness or injury for an extended period.
- The length of the leave of absence is determined by the Company according to length of service.

## Workplace Conduct

### Duties
- Employees shall perform their duties faithfully and follow the instructions of their supervisors.

### Rules of Conduct
- Employees shall observe the following rules:
  - Do not use the Company's equipment for personal purposes without permission.
  - Do not accept gifts or other benefits in connection with their duties.
  - Do not damage the Company's reputation.
  - Do not engage in other employment without the Company's permission.

### Prohibition of Power Harassment
- Employees shall not harm the working environment of other employees through words or conduct that exceed what is necessary and appropriate for business, taking advantage of a superior position.

### Prohibition of Sexual Harassment
- Employees shall not engage in sexual conduct that disadvantages other employees or harms their working environment.

### Confidentiality
- Employees shall not disclose to any third party confidential information of the Company or its customers obtained in the course of their duties, during or after employment.

## Working Hours, Breaks and Days Off

### Working Hours
- Working hours are 40 hours per week and 8 hours per day, excluding breaks.
- The start and end times are 9:00 a.m. and 6:00 p.m.

### Breaks
- Employees are given a one-hour break from 12:00 p.m. to 1:00 p.m.

### Days Off
- Days off are as follows:
  - Saturdays and Sundays
  - Public holidays
  - The year-end and New Year holidays (December 29 to January 3)

### Overtime
- The Company may require employees to work overtime or on days off within the limits of the overtime agreement with the employee representative.

## Leave

### Annual Paid Leave
- Employees who have worked for six months and attended at least 80 percent of working days are granted 10 days of annual paid leave.

### Hourly Paid Leave
- Employees may take up to five days of annual paid leave per year in units of one hour.

### Maternity Leave
- An employee who is expected to give birth within six weeks may take maternity leave upon request.

### Parental and Family Care Leave
- Employees who are raising a child or caring for a family member may take parental leave or family care leave as provided in separate rules.

### Fertility Treatment Leave
- Employees undergoing fertility treatment may take up to 5 days of leave per year upon request.

### Bereavement Leave {#bereavement}
- Employees may take the following bereavement leave upon request:
  - Upon the employee's own marriage: 5 days
  - When the employee's spouse gives birth: 2 days
  - Upon the death of the employee's spouse, child or parent: 5 days
  - Upon the death of a sibling, grandparent, or parent or sibling of the spouse: 2 days

### Sick Leave
- Employees who need medical treatment for a personal injury or illness and cannot reasonably be expected to work are granted 5 days of sick leave per year.

### Jury Duty Leave
- Employees who serve as jurors or are summoned as prospective jurors are granted leave as follows:
  - When serving as a juror: the necessary number of days
  - When summoned as a prospective juror: the necessary number of hours
- Employees who stand as candidates in a public election are granted the leave necessary for the election campaign.

## Wages

### Composition of Wages
- Wages consist of the following:
  - Base pay
  - Allowances
    - Family allowance
    - Commuting allowance
    - Position allowance
  - Overtime pay

### Base Pay
- Base pay is determined individually, taking into account the employee's duties, skills, performance and experience.

### Family Allowance
- A family allowance is paid to employees who support the following family members:
  - For each child under 18: $5,000 per year
  - For each parent aged 65 or over: $3,000 per year

### Commuting Allowance
- A commuting allowance is paid up to $500 per month for the actual cost of commuting.

### Overtime Pay
- Overtime pay is paid for work beyond the working hours in [[scope]] and for work on days off, at the rates required by law.

## Retirement and Dismissal

### Retirement Age
- The retirement age is 65.

### Resignation
- An employee who wishes to resign shall notify the Company at least 14 days in advance.

### Dismissal {#dismissal}
- The Company may dismiss an employee if the employee is unable to perform their duties because of a physical or mental disability, or if the employee's performance is significantly poor and not expected to improve.

## Safety and Health

### Health Checkups
- The Company provides a health checkup for employees once a year.

### Workplace Safety
- Employees shall follow the Company's safety instructions and help prevent accidents in the workplace.
