# Riverside City Home Repair Grant Guidelines

## General Provisions

### Purpose {#purpose}
- These Guidelines set out the procedures for grants that Riverside City (the "City") awards to owners who repair vacant homes for reuse.

### Definitions
- In these Guidelines:
  - "Vacant Home" means a house in the City that has been unoccupied for one year or more.
  - "Applicant" means the owner of a Vacant Home who applies for a grant.

## Grants

### Eligible Work
- Eligible work is repair work on a Vacant Home carried out by a contractor with a place of business in the City.

### Amount of the Grant {#amount}
- The grant is half of the cost of the eligible work, up to $5,000.

### Application
- An Applicant shall submit an application to the Mayor before starting the work.
- The Mayor shall decide whether to award the grant within 30 days after receiving the application.

### Reporting
- A grant recipient shall submit a completion report within 30 days after the work is completed.

## Supplementary Provisions {supplement}

### Effective Date
- These Guidelines take effect on April 1, 2026.
- The amendments to these Guidelines take effect on October 1, 2026.
