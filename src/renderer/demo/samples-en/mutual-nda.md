# Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement

This Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into as of April 1, 2026 between Acme Corp., a Delaware corporation ("Acme"), and Northwind Traders Ltd., a company incorporated in England and Wales ("Northwind") (each, a "Party" and together, the "Parties").

## Definitions and Interpretation

### Definitions {#def}
- In this Agreement:
  - "Confidential Information" means all information disclosed by a Party (the "Disclosing Party") to the other Party (the "Receiving Party"), whether orally or in writing, that is designated as confidential or that reasonably should be understood to be confidential.
  - "Purpose" means evaluating a potential business relationship between the Parties.
  - "Representatives" means a Party's directors, officers, employees and professional advisers.
- Headings are for convenience only and do not affect interpretation.

### Exclusions {#exclusions}
- Confidential Information does not include information that:
  - is or becomes publicly available through no fault of the Receiving Party;
  - was lawfully known to the Receiving Party before disclosure; or
  - is independently developed by the Receiving Party without use of the Confidential Information.

## Obligations

### Use and Disclosure {#use}
- The Receiving Party shall use the Confidential Information solely for the Purpose.
- The Receiving Party may disclose Confidential Information only to its Representatives who need to know it for the Purpose and who are bound by obligations of confidentiality no less protective than those in this Agreement.

### Compelled Disclosure
- If the Receiving Party is required by law to disclose any Confidential Information, it shall give the Disclosing Party prompt written notice, except for information described in [[exclusions:1:2]].

### Return of Materials
- Upon the Disclosing Party's written request, the Receiving Party shall promptly return or destroy all Confidential Information in its possession.

## Term and General

### Term {#term}
- This Agreement remains in effect for two years from the date first written above.
- The obligations under [[use]] survive for three years after expiry or termination of this Agreement.

### Remedies
- Each Party acknowledges that a breach of [[use]] may cause irreparable harm, and that the Disclosing Party is entitled to seek injunctive relief in addition to any other remedy.

### Governing Law
- This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of New York.
